Stream settings

How these settings work

Rolling attendance counts streams attended within the latest window (for example, 3 of the last 5); those streams do not have to be consecutive. Consecutive attendance requires a streak of attended streams. Older attendance already discarded cannot be recovered, so larger windows and streaks become reliable as new streams are recorded.

New settings affect ongoing grouping and presence. Existing reminders keep their original expiry, and finalized awards are not rewritten. Match scores affect new checks and searches, not past moderation; adjust them carefully.
